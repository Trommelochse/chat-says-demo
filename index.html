Questions

Does Chat Says touch my video?

No. It never sees, receives or sends your video. The dock reads chat. The overlay is a see-through web page with just the poll or rating card. OBS puts it on top of your stream, on your own computer.

Does it post in my chat?

No. It only reads. It can't post, ban, time out, or change anything on your channel.

Do my viewers need to install anything?

No. No extension, no app, no account with us. They chat like they always do, on any device.

I use Streamlabs. Does it work?

The overlay, yes: add it as a Browser source. Streamlabs has no custom docks, so open the dock link in a browser tab instead.

What if the AI isn't sure what someone meant?

Then the message counts as “none”. A skipped message is better than a wrong vote. You can see every label in the demo above.

Can someone get a rude message onto my overlay?

The overlay only quotes a message now and then, and a filter keeps slurs and personal info off it.

Twitch only? What about Kick and YouTube?

Twitch first. Kick is next. YouTube comes later.

Does it keep my chat logs?

No. Chat is read during your stream to count votes and catch questions. It isn't stored after the stream.
